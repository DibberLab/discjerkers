Official rules of the Disc Jerkers

01

The Ace Policy

Aces on holes shorter than 200 feet do not count. Unless you stepped four feet behind the tee pad to make it 201.

02

Putting Etiquette

If an opponent takes longer than 45 seconds to prepare a putt, you are legally permitted to finish an entire nine-hole round while they prep.

03

Grip Lock Rights

Grip lock is universally acknowledged as the worst part of disc golf. It beats ankle bee stings, lost cicadas, and the porta-potties at Memorial.

04

Equipment Justification

Any missed seven-foot putt is 100% the fault of the plastic, requiring the immediate purchase of four new versatility putters.

05

The 9-Hole Clause

Nine-hole wins are not recognized. A win is only recorded after eighteen holes of suffering.

06

E-Commerce Integrity

The official store must collect your card, crash at checkout, and ship the order anyway without ever emailing you. We have regrettably fixed this. Stripe sends a receipt now. We are sorry.

07

The Bag Strike Exemption

A disc that comes to rest by rolling into your own bag is not out of bounds, not a penalty stroke, and not grounds for a rules debate. The bag was left in play. It assumed the risk.

08

The Marebear Mando

Every player in the group must, at least once per round, throw a disc between Marebear's legs. This is a live mando. Miss the gap and you're re-teeing, no matter how good a girl she was about it.
